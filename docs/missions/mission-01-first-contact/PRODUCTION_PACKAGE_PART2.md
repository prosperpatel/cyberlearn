## ARIA — CHARACTER BRIEF

**Full Name:** Adaptive Response Intelligence Assistant (ARIA)  
**Voice Cast Direction:** A human voice actor, not synthetic. Female voice preferred. Range: warm baritone-adjacent alto — think Viola Davis warmth with Cate Blanchett precision. Never sounds like Alexa, Siri, or a text-to-speech engine. Never sounds like a teacher. Always sounds like a colleague who knows more than you but respects you anyway.

**Voice Processing:** Minimal. A very slight presence room (small, hard surfaces — a glass office). A hint of high-frequency shimmer on the 4–8kHz range (gives her a slightly non-standard vocal quality without being robotic). No heavy processing. She sounds like a person.

**Character Traits in Voice:**
- Does not rush
- Does not fill silence nervously
- Her silences are as intentional as her words
- Uses the learner's title ("Analyst") exactly three times in the mission: once at the very start, once at the "good work" moment, once at the very end. The title carries weight because it is used sparingly.
- Never performatively enthusiastic
- Her authority comes from precision, not volume

## GLOBAL INTERACTION RULES

**Click targets:** Minimum 44×44px. Preferred 48×48px or larger for primary actions.  
**Hover response time:** ≤ 150ms for all visual state changes.  
**Transition duration:** 200–400ms for state changes. 500–800ms for component entrances. Never over 1000ms for feedback.  
**Keyboard focus ring:** 2px solid `#00D9FF`, offset 2px. Never suppressed unless replaced with an equivalent focus indicator.  
**Touch targets:** Minimum 44×44px with 8px spacing between adjacent targets.  
**Idle behavior:** After 90 seconds of complete inactivity in a scene, a subtle "still there?" pulse plays on the most logical next action (primary button or primary interactive element). After 180 seconds, auto-pause activates with an overlay: "Paused — click to resume."

## RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR

**Viewport breakpoints:**

| Breakpoint | Width | Behavior |
|------------|-------|----------|
| `xs` | < 480px | Single column; terminal takes full width; ARIA panel becomes top overlay; CIA cards stack vertically |
| `sm` | 480–768px | Same as xs but with more padding |
| `md` | 768–1024px | Two-column layout becomes available for debrief split view |
| `lg` | 1024–1440px | Standard layout as designed |
| `xl` | > 1440px | Content max-width constrained to 1440px, centered |

**Mobile-specific:**
- HUD height: 40px (vs 48px desktop)
- Touch targets all ≥ 44px
- Drag interactions replaced by tap-to-select (see Section 6)
- Terminal font size: 11px (vs 13px)
- Reduced motion applied by default on devices with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`
- Full-screen mode recommended (and prompted) on mobile
